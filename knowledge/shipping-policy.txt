SHIPPING POLICY

Domestic Shipping (India):
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days (Free on orders above Rs. 999)
- Express delivery: 1-2 business days (Rs. 199 flat fee)
- Same-day delivery: Available in metro cities (Rs. 399 flat fee, order before 2PM)

International Shipping:
- Available to 25+ countries
- Standard: 7-14 business days
- Express: 3-5 business days
- Customs duties and taxes are customer's responsibility

Tracking:
- Tracking link is sent via email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch
- You can track your order in Account > Orders > Track
- If tracking shows "no movement" for 48 hours, contact support

Delivery Issues:
- Lost packages: We reship or refund within 7 days of confirmation
- Damaged packages: Photo evidence required, refund issued same day
- Wrong address: Customer is responsible if delivery attempt already made

Order Modifications:
- Address change: Allowed within 12 hours of order placement
- Cancellation: Allowed until the order is dispatched (typically 4-6 hours)

Holiday Delays:
- Expect 2-3 day delays during Diwali, Christmas, and New Year
- We proactively notify customers about expected delays

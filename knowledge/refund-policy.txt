REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Eligibility:
- Refunds available within 7 days of purchase for most products
- Digital products and services are non-refundable once delivered
- Defective items: refund or replacement at customer choice

How to request a refund:
1. Sign in to your account
2. Go to Settings > Orders
3. Find the order and click "Request Refund"
4. Select reason and submit
5. You'll get a confirmation email within 1 hour

Processing times:
- UPI / wallets: 1-3 business days
- Credit/debit cards: 5-7 business days
- Net banking: 3-5 business days
- Refunds are always sent to the original payment method

Cancellations:
- Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of placement if not yet dispatched
- After dispatch, refuse delivery and we'll process a refund upon receipt
- Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period

Partial refunds:
- For bundle products, partial refund based on item value
- Shipping fees are non-refundable for change-of-mind returns
- Promotional discounts are adjusted proportionally

Disputes:
- If you don't see your refund within the stated timeline, contact support with your order ID
- Chargebacks should be a last resort — we'll always try to resolve first
- For bank disputes, please allow 30 days for the bank's investigation

Contact:
- Refund support: refunds@ticketdesk.io
- Include order ID and payment reference for fastest resolution
- Phone support for refunds: 1800-XXX-XXXX (Mon-Fri, 9AM-6PM IST)
